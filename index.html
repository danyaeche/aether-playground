Meet the classics.

From cloud-soft cornicione to crisp, caramelized edges—every style has its own rules, rituals, and reasons to love it.

Neapolitan

Cloud-soft crust, charred leopard spots, sweet San Marzano tomato, and milky fior di latte.

02
THIN / CRISP

Rome, Italy · Thin & crackly

Roman Tonda

Paper-thin and audibly crisp from center to edge, with a restrained hand on the toppings.

03
FOLD IT

New York, USA · The fold

New York

A broad, foldable slice with low-moisture mozzarella, tomato tang, and a crisp-chewy undercarriage.

04
SICILIA
SFINCIONE

Palermo, Italy · Airy & golden

Sicilian

Focaccia-like dough with a golden bottom, plush crumb, tomato, onion, herbs, and pecorino.

05
FRICO EDGE

Detroit, USA · Caramelized edge

Detroit

Cheese all the way to the steel pan creates a lacy frico crown around a light, focaccia-like center.

06
CHICAGO
DEEP DISH

Chicago, USA · Fork required

Deep Dish

A buttery, high-walled crust layered with mozzarella, fillings, and chunky tomato—in that order.

07
COAL FIRED

New Haven, USA · Charred & oblong

New Haven Apizza

A coal-fired, delightfully charred crust with an irregular shape and a signature smoky chew.

08
BARI
PUGLIA

Bari, Italy · Olive oil crunch

Focaccia Barese

A dimpled semolina dough with cherry tomatoes, olives, oregano, and a generous olive-oil crunch.
